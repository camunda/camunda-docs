Create and manage your organization's users in Camunda Hub.

## Add a user

To add a user to your organization in Camunda Hub SaaS:

1. In the left navigation, click **Organization > Manage organization**.
1. On the **Users** tab, click **Add new user**.
1. Enter an **Email address** and select one or more [user roles](../../hub/organization/users-and-roles.md#roles-and-permissions).
1. Click **Send invite**.

## Edit or remove a user

To edit or remove a user in your organization:

1. In the left navigation, click **Organization > Manage organization**.
1. On the **Users** tab, on a user row, open the vertical ellipsis menu.
1. Click **Edit user** or **Remove**.

## Limitations

The number of users that can be part of an organization depends on the plan you use.

## Restrictions

In Enterprise plans, the hostname section of the email address for invites can be restricted to meet your internal security policies. [Contact Camunda support](https://camunda.com/services/support/) to get this configured according to your needs.
